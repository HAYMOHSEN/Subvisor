Summary

Subvisor – Subscription Trimmer (“Subvisor”) is a desktop application for Windows that helps you keep track of your own subscriptions. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your computer.

What Subvisor collects

Nothing. Subvisor has no user accounts, does not connect to banks or payment providers, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send any data to Jotech or to any third party.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter — service names, prices, dates, notes, links — is stored only on your computer, in the app’s local application-data folder. You can export it as CSV, back it up as a JSON file, or delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data from Windows Settings.

Private details (license keys and logins)

If you choose to store license keys, account logins or other private information with a subscription, Subvisor encrypts them using the Windows Data Protection API, bound to your Windows user account, before writing them to disk. They are masked on screen, never included in CSV exports, and cannot be read by another Windows account or another PC. The optional “Export as plain text” action writes them unencrypted to a file you choose, and only after you confirm it.

Internet access

Subvisor works fully offline. The only time it opens a network connection is when you click a “Manage / cancel” link you added yourself; that link opens in your default browser, outside the app.

Notifications

Renewal reminders are generated locally on your PC using Windows notifications. No notification content leaves your device.

Permissions

The app may request to run at Windows start-up so reminders can be shown while it sits in the system tray. You can turn this off inside the app or in Windows Settings → Apps → Startup.

Children

Subvisor is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
